## Archive-wide repeated patterns

- **Brand shell:** A near-black or blue-black header anchors the gold shield,
  `GemReserve.io` wordmark, tracked `OWN. TRADE. REDEEM.` line, uppercase
  dropdown navigation, Login, and a gold waitlist action. Most informational
  boards add a slim company announcement strip; the asset catalog uses a
  more compact header variant. A dense multi-column footer repeats throughout.
- **Typography and palette:** High-contrast editorial serif headings pair with
  compact sans-serif body and UI copy. Warm metallic gold, ivory, muted gray,
  and dark teal sit over black; gemstone-detail templates replace teal with a
  stone-specific accent such as ruby red, emerald green, aquamarine cyan, or
  charoite purple.
- **Section grammar:** Thin gold rules, small diamond ornaments, uppercase
  section titles, one-pixel bordered panels, restrained corner radii, and tight
  modular grids make unrelated page types feel like one system.
- **Imagery:** Heroes are generally copy-left and image-right, with dark
  gradients preserving legibility over macro gemstones, rough stones, shields,
  vaults, tweezers, loupes, certificates, cards, dashboards, and devices.
- **Trust and data modules:** Gold line icons, short trust propositions,
  numbered process steps, metric strips, comparison tables, verification
  records, and proof/custody panels recur across platform, asset, enterprise,
  and investor pages.
- **Catalog/detail families:** Discovery boards use repeated gemstone cards and
  filters. Polished and rough detail boards share a repeatable hero, facts,
  provenance/quality, verification, market/value, chart, and action-panel
  structure, with the chosen stone controlling the accent color.
- **Conversion pattern:** Wide bordered CTAs repeatedly combine a crest,
  gemstone or vault composition, compact assurance copy, and a filled gold
  button for exploration, contact, redemption, documents, or waitlist entry.
- **Responsive pressure points:** The desktop boards are unusually dense. The
  full navigation, five-plus-column grids, small type, long timelines, wide data
  tables, overlapping hero art, and multi-column footer all require deliberate
  reflow. The four taller/narrower enterprise boards also show that the archive
  does not use one uniform canvas aspect ratio.

## Shipping boundary

The raw boards and all working reference crops live under `reference/`. That
directory is gitignored and is not shipped with the site; only reconstructed,
optimized production assets placed under `public/` are eligible for delivery.
